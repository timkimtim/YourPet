# Changelog

## 0.2.3

- Give Persik animated posture changes between sleeping, sitting, and standing. Approaching with the cursor wakes him and leaving lets him settle into his previous rest.
- Jump with a single left click and keep dragging as a separate gesture.
- Crossfade animation changes for pets without dedicated posture clips.

## 0.2.2

- Track fullscreen windows on the pet's own monitor, including when focus moves to another screen.
- Check the destination monitor after the pet is dragged between displays.

## 0.2.1

- Match Persik's cloudy left eye to the original drawing across the animation frames and app icon.
- Add permanent download links for the latest Windows x64 and ARM64 builds.

## 0.2.0

- Persik jumps away into a vertical portal when entering fullscreen and jumps out toward the viewer when returning.
- Exclusive fullscreen Direct3D games still hide Persik immediately. The tray menu can turn transition animations off independently of fullscreen hiding.
- Custom pet packages can provide their own optional fullscreen transition clips.

## 0.1.0

- Portable Windows 11 builds for x64 and ARM64.
- Persik is included as the default pet and example package.
- Pet packages can define named clips, several idle poses, walking, jumping, and 4, 8, or 16 gaze directions.
- Tray controls for resizing, always-on-top, click-through, fullscreen hiding, and launch at sign-in.
